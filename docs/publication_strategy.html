1 Executive Overview: Why a Multi-Variant Portfolio Wins

Because this work bridges applied engineering & ECE, formal verification (Lean 4), empirical software engineering, and AI-for-science metascience, no single venue maximizes speed, engineering readership, and archival rigor simultaneously. Late-2026 Lean adoption is also moving rapidly (a 6.3× rate jump and 32× codebase-size jump in six months), meaning a 14-month journal cycle alone would sacrifice timeliness.

Fast Broad Impact 2–4 Months

Trim the pilot report into a punchy CACM or IEEE Software / IEEE Computer article pairing the longitudinal inflection curve with an actionable engineering playbook (P1–P4 & Five-Gate verification checklist), alongside an empirically framed arXiv preprint.

Formal-Methods Flagship Feb 2027

Add Docker-sandboxed lake build + #print axioms verification on compact repos and human inter-rater reliability (IRR) on the 20 Layer-A audits to target ITP 2027 (Glasgow; deadline ~late Feb 2027) and AITP 2027.

Archival / Benchmark Anchor Spring 2027

Add a Q1/Q2 2027 slice (T3), a cs.LG/stat.ML arm, and a Croissant/Zenodo benchmark dataset of verified LaTeX↔Lean drift pairs for NeurIPS 2027 D&B (~May 2027) or EMSE / ACM TOSEM.

3 AI-Use Policies & Compliance Matrix

Major publishers evaluate two distinct AI roles separately: (1) AI as a research instrument—here, the flash/pro agents in workflows/classify_candidates.py and workflows/audit_layer_a.py—and (2) AI-assisted drafting reviewed, verified, and edited by human authors. Every publisher below permits both roles under strict human accountability and venue-specific disclosure rules. No venue allows listing an AI system as an author.

⚠ Two Critical arXiv 2026 Enforcement Traps to Fix Before Any Preprint Upload
  1. arXiv cs.* Ban on Un-Refereed "Surveys" & Position Papers: arXiv Computer Science categories now require documented prior peer review for literature surveys and position papers. Action: Do not title or frame the preprint as "A Longitudinal Survey..."—frame it accurately as an empirical longitudinal measurement and semantic-fidelity audit study of 9,837 preprints and 20 codebases.
  2. 1-Year arXiv Ban for Unverified LLM Artifacts (Immediate BibTeX Red Flag!): arXiv enforces 1-year bans for incontrovertible evidence of unchecked LLM output—especially hallucinated or placeholder citations. In papers/shared/references.bib#L34-L56, three background citations currently contain placeholder author strings: {Lean Atlas Authors} (2604.16347), {Axle Authors} (2606.26442), and {ProofBridge Authors} (2510.15681). Every single entry in references.bib must be verified 1-by-1 against live arXiv/DOI metadata before submission anywhere.

  • LLM-as-a-judge hygiene: Archive exact model versions, full prompt templates from workflows/audit_layer_a.py, raw traces, and (for EMSE/TOSEM) an open-weight judge baseline on a subset.

5 Critical Pre-Submission Risks & Hygiene Checklist

Because this paper critiques Process Opacity (Mode #12) and unverified AI outputs, reviewers will hold this manuscript to an unusually high standard of transparency and verification.

  • [URGENT] Audit & Fix All BibTeX Entries in papers/shared/references.bib: Replace placeholder authors ({Lean Atlas Authors}, {Axle Authors}, {ProofBridge Authors} at lines 34–56) and verify every eprint ID, title, and author list across all 54 citations against live arXiv/DOI records to eliminate any risk of arXiv moderation bans or reviewer flags.
  • [URGENT] Human-Verify Every Named Negative Audit Finding & Pin Commit SHAs: Before publishing critiques of named preprints—especially 2611.26272 (Paper2Lean / OptPaper-20), 2611.04876, 2605.14610, and 2605.02856 (see reports/slices/2026-11/phase2_layer_a_audit.md and reports/slices/2026-05/phase2_layer_a_audit.md)—human authors must directly verify every cited file:line claim, record exact arXiv versions (v1) and Git commit SHAs, maintain strictly factual tone, and consider courtesy pre-publication notification to those authors.
  • Add a Transparent AI-Use & Human-Accountability Statement: Draft venue-tailored disclosures covering (a) exact LLM versions, dates, and prompts used in Stages 4–5 and (b) AI-assisted manuscript drafting reviewed, fact-checked, and edited by human authors (formatted per ACM Methods, IEEE Acknowledgments, Springer Amber Tier, or Elsevier Declaration template).
  • Human IRR Calibration for LLM-as-a-Judge (Required for SE / Archival Venues): Independently human-annotate a stratified sample of Stage-4 classifications and Stage-5 Layer-A audits; report Cohen's κ / Krippendorff's α and reconcile disagreements transparently.
  • Double-Blind & Anonymization Hygiene (ITP / NeurIPS / ASE): Strip USC ANRG attribution, author footnotes, and direct non-anonymous GitHub URLs from double-blind submissions; host an anonymized replication snapshot (Anonymous GitHub or anonymized Zenodo deposit).
  • Third-Party Code Licensing in Replication Packages: Do not redistribute cloned third-party GitHub .lean source trees inside Zenodo archives unless their licenses explicitly permit it. Distribute your own pipeline scripts, SQLite metadata schemas, repo URLs + commit SHAs, prompt rubrics, and audit JSON outputs.
  • Clean Self-Plagiarism Boundaries Across Variants: Non-archival outputs (arXiv empirical preprint, AITP 2027 abstract, MATH-AI workshop) do not block archival publication. Pairing a short practitioner magazine article (CACM / IEEE Software) with an expanded archival journal/benchmark paper (EMSE / TOSEM / NeurIPS D&B / ITP) is standard when clearly cross-cited and substantially extended (≥30–50% new empirical scope such as sandboxed builds, human IRR, cs.LG, and T3).
