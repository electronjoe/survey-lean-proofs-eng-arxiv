## 1. Executive Summary

To empirically test Scott Moeller's Track 3 question — *whether our 12-mode failure taxonomy (`Table 1` in `main.tex`, derived from 2025–2026 AI mathematical-proof postmortems) transfers to Lean 4 software, database, hardware, and protocol operational-correctness papers* — we audited **all 8 `operational_system_fv` papers** across our complete 15-category longitudinal corpus (`T0 + T1 + T2`, `n = 82` confirmed Lean papers): **4 locally cloned repositories inspected at the `file:line` level** (`2611.10810`, `2611.19551`, `2605.16407`, `2605.19531`) plus **4 prose-only papers lacking accessible public Lean artifacts** (`2611.20298`, `2611.25599`, `2511.15517`, and retroactive T1 `v2` `cs.PF` cross-list `2605.01140` *SoCal*).

Five concrete empirical findings justify an explicit **External-Validity Scope Boundary** in Sections 2 and 9 of `main.tex` (and fixed two real false-positive bugs in our own Stage-3/4 pipeline before Phase C1):

1. **Table 1's Math-Proof Layer-A Rubric Misfires Systematically on Zero-Dependency Operational FV (`2/4` Cloned Repos Falsely Flagged `Mode3_CustomDefs`):**
   - Both **`2611.10810`** (DBLog CDC, `10,862` LoC, `507/507` axiom-clean theorems in `11.15s` Docker build, dual-kernel Isabelle/HOL + Lean 4) and **`2605.19531`** (Conflict-Freedom PODC, `34,257` LoC, `2,061` theorems across 101 modules with repo-native `KernelAudit.lean`) were statically flagged **`Mode3_CustomDefs`** solely because they intentionally use **zero-dependency Lean 4 core (`0` Mathlib imports)** to model CDC logs and concurrent shared-memory executions where no Mathlib abstraction exists.
   - In addition, **`2605.19531`** tripped the math-proof `>20k LoC` **`Mode10_ProofBloat`** heuristic despite clean modular decomposition across full safety + liveness + machine semantics, and our own `src/patterns.py` static scanner falsely counted **`admit: 4`** on **`2611.19551`** (TrustBound) because its payment-admission transition constructor is named `| admit (s : Epoch)` (`formal/Closure.lean:111,124,144,215`)!
2. **Yet Layer B/C Modes (`Mode6`, `Mode12`) + Layer A `Mode4` Vacuity Transfer Sharply — Exposing a Provably Inconsistent Axiom in `2605.16407`:**
   - Code-auditing **`2605.16407`** (*Proof-Carrying Certificates for Generative AI*, `43` Mathlib imports, `2,381` LoC) revealed **18 custom `axiom` declarations**: 8 ML/crypto/distributional oracles, 7 worked-example axioms, and **3 purely mathematical axioms postulated despite importing Mathlib** (`cauchy_schwarz_sq` at `Certificate.lean:26`, `innerProd_sub` at `Certificate.lean:31`, `HoeffdingInequality` at `Concentration.lean:44`).
   - Crucially, **`innerProd_sub` is provably inconsistent (derives `False`)**: because `Embedding := List Rat` (`Basic.lean:17`) and `innerProd` (`Basic.lean:29-32`) + `List.zipWith` truncate mismatched-length lists, `innerProd_sub [1] [] [1]` asserts **`(0 : Rat) = 1`**!
   - Furthermore, **>12 headline "theorems"** in `2605.16407` are 1-line projections of proof fields already assumed inside the certificate `structure` (`CEGAR.lean:55-58` `cegar_terminates`, `Action.lean:80-102`, `ScopeOfValidity.lean:39-43`, `Concentration.lean:117-120`, `Additional.lean:44,67,115,136,210`, and `MCR.lean:78-82` where `MCRCertificate` omits the `p_maximal_over_all` field claimed in its own docstring at `MCR.lean:19-20`).
   - Meanwhile, **`Mode12_ProcessOpacity`** is even more acute in operational FV than in engineering math proofs: **4/8 (50.0%)** of operational-FV papers (`2611.20298`, `2611.25599`, `2511.15517`, `2605.01140`) have no accessible public Lean repository.
3. **The Dominant Risks in Operational FV Are the 5 Modes *Missing* from Table 1:**
   - **OpFV-1 (Model-to-Implementation Refinement Gap) — `8/8` (100%):** Even the two highest-rigor `Tier3_Gold` operational-FV repos (`2611.10810` DBLog CDC `README.md:117`; `2611.19551` TrustBound `README.md:73-74`, `research/service_refinement/README.md:129-134`) verify abstract transition models with **zero universal refinement proof** to Debezium/Flink/PostgreSQL or `stripe_gate.py`. `2605.19531` assumes atomic wait-free snapshot objects rather than proving read/write register refinement (`MODEL.md:12-14`, `README.md:102-107`), and `2605.01140` (*SoCal*) proves type-safety for an abstract typed IR without verifying its Colobus-to-C compiler.
   - **OpFV-2 (Unproved Environment / Fault / Adversary Assumptions) — `8/8` (100%):** Every paper relies on substantial unverified deployment assumptions (DBLog's in-order transport, query visibility, single restart; TrustBound's A1–A5 host isolation, total mediation, truthful provider; `2605.16407`'s 8 ML/crypto oracles; Tether's syscall-to-action sandbox inference; SoCal's unverified Colobus-to-C lowering and Gibbon runtime).
   - **OpFV-3 (Safety-Without-Liveness) — `4/8` lack mechanized Lean liveness:** `2611.10810` delegates liveness to bounded 2-key TLA+ TLC (`tla/README.md:48-51,88`); `2611.19551` explicitly proves safety only (`formal/Admission.lean:11`); `2611.25599` proves safety invariants only (Table 1); `2605.16407` models no CEGAR loop. By contrast, **`2605.19531`** is the exemplary counter-case proving *both* infinite-history linearizability (safety) and conflict-freedom progress (liveness), and `2611.10810`, `2611.19551`, and `2605.19531` all combat trace vacuity with explicit execution witnesses and/or premise-removal mutations (`8` rejected mutations in TrustBound!).
   - **OpFV-4 (Extraction / Monitor / Cross-Kernel TCB Gaps) — `5/8`:** Tether's Lean EDSL-to-monitor compilation & syscall front-end; TrustBound's trusted Python trace-projection checker (`trace_evidence.py`); Beluga's manual Rust deployment; SoCal's manual Gibbon/C Colobus compiler; and DBLog's cross-kernel scope gap (`Orphan_Absence_Range_Merge` is Isabelle-only, omitted from the Lean 4 port, `lean/README.md:373-376`).
   - **OpFV-5 (Evolution & Maintenance Debt) — `3/8` concrete signals:** TrustBound's checked trace suite already drifted from 66 → 64 transitions after one guard change (`research/service_refinement/README.md:15-18`); DBLog maintains three parallel formalisms (Isabelle/HOL + Lean 4 + TLA+) with 4 unfixed TLA+ review limitations (`tla/README.md:121-143`); Conflict-Freedom spans `34.3k` LoC / 101 files.
4. **Authoring Paradigms (`P1`–`P4`) Do Not Fit Operational FV:** `0/8` use `P2`, `P3` (blueprints), or `P4` (`Challenge.lean`/`comparator`). Their actual paradigms are **Abstract Design-Model / Core-Calculus Verification (`6/8`)**, **Executable Embedded Policy DSL (`1/8`: `2611.25599`)**, and **Certificate-Schema Oracle Wrapper (`1/8`: `2605.16407`)**.
5. **Pipeline Fixes Landed Before Phase C1:** Updated [`workflows/classify_candidates.py`](../workflows/classify_candidates.py) (scoping `Mode3_CustomDefs` strictly to math/algorithmic papers, adding `OpFV_RefinementGap` & `OpFV_SafetyOnlyNoLiveness`, and adding the Track 1 4-way `role_4way` + `verification_object` fields) and tightened `admit` tactic detection in [`src/patterns.py`](../src/patterns.py) with a regression test in [`tests/test_patterns.py`](../tests/test_patterns.py).

## 3. Code-Level Deep Dive on the Key Empirical Surprises

### 3.1 `2605.16407` — Inconsistent Custom Axiom (`innerProd_sub`) & Tautological Struct Projections
- **Provably inconsistent axiom (`False` derivable):** In `EmbeddingSensitivity/Basic.lean:17,29-32`, `Embedding := List Rat` and `innerProd` stops at the shorter list (`| [], _ => 0 | _, [] => 0`). In `EmbeddingSensitivity/Certificate.lean:31-33`, `axiom innerProd_sub (u v w : Embedding) : innerProd (List.zipWith (· - ·) u v) w = innerProd u w - innerProd v w` omits any length-equality guard. Instantiating `u := [1]`, `v := []`, `w := [1]` yields `List.zipWith (· - ·) [1] [] = []`, so LHS `= innerProd [] [1] = 0` while RHS `= innerProd [1] [1] - innerProd [] [1] = 1 - 0 = 1` — proving **`0 = 1` (`False`)** in the imported environment!
- **Three unproved mathematical theorems postulated despite `43` Mathlib imports:** `cauchy_schwarz_sq` (`Certificate.lean:26-27`), `innerProd_sub` (`Certificate.lean:31-33`), and `HoeffdingInequality : List Bool → Nat → Rat → Rat → Prop` (`Concentration.lean:44-45`, an uninterpreted predicate unconnected to $2\exp(-2k\varepsilon^2)$).
- **Struct-field projection "theorems":** `cegar_terminates := cegar.p_within_budget` (`CEGAR.lean:55-58`), `step_post_holds`/`step_executor_matches`/`trajectory_all_steps_safe` (`Action.lean:80-102`), `all_oracles_in_scope := cert.p_scopes_hold s h_mem` (`ScopeOfValidity.lean:39-43`), `hoeffding_confidence_interval := cert.h_hoeffding_valid` (`Concentration.lean:117-120`), `output_avoids_forbidden`/`intervention_bounded_change`/`temporal_still_valid`/`budget_respected`/`kernel_signer_approved` (`Additional.lean:44,67,115,136,210`), and `MCR.lean:78-82` (`residue_is_fixed_point := cert.p_residue_cert`, where `MCRCertificate` at lines 37–49 omits the `p_maximal_over_all` maximality field claimed in its docstring at lines 19–20).

### 3.2 `2611.10810` & `2605.19531` — Why Zero-Mathlib Operational FV Breaks `Mode3_CustomDefs`
- Both repositories demonstrate that **zero external dependencies (`0` Mathlib imports) is a *best practice*, not a red flag, for operational systems/DB/concurrency verification**:
  - `2611.10810` intentionally builds on Lean 4 core alone (`lean/README.md:382-395`) to keep the TCB minimal and compile 507 theorems in `11.15s` as an independent second kernel alongside Isabelle/HOL.
  - `2605.19531` intentionally builds on Lean 4 Std alone (`README.md:52-53`) with an exhaustive `CFLeanProof/KernelAudit.lean` (`lines 14-43`) that fails CI if *any* file in `CFLeanProof/` is unimported or *any* `ConflictFreedom.*` declaration uses a non-standard axiom.
- Treating absence of Mathlib imports as `Mode3_CustomDefs` ("definition smuggling") conflates *unanchored continuous math definitions* (where standard Mathlib definitions like `ConvexOn`, `Measure`, or `Matrix` exist and custom re-definitions risk subtle mismatches) with *inherently domain-specific system transition models* (CDC logs, payment epochs, shared-memory snapshots).

### 3.3 `2611.19551` — Operational-FV Validation Techniques Beyond Math-Proof Tooling
- TrustBound (`2611.19551`) showcases two verification practices characteristic of mature operational FV that have no counterpart in our `P1`–`P4` math-proof paradigms:
  1. **Premise-removal mutation testing** (`README.md:111-112`, `evidence/core/`): 8 deliberately broken transition-system mutations verified to be *rejected* by Lean, ruling out vacuous/tautological invariants.
  2. **Trace-projection conformance checking** (`research/service_refinement/check_traces.py`): projecting 6 real SQLite `StripeGate` runs (64 transitions) into Lean `ExternalRuns` terms and checking closure — a pragmatic bridge across OpFV-1, while transparently acknowledging it does not constitute universal refinement (`README.md:73-74`, `docs/CLAIMS.md:81-83`).
