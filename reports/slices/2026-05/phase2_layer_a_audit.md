- **Executive Verdict:** arXiv:2605.14610 presents a statistical/signal-processing method (PATP) with a small, clean, zero-sorry Lean 4 + Mathlib v4.26.0 supplement (SZabolotnii/Ku-PATP-code-supplement: 8 files, 473 non-empty LOC, 25 theorems, 11 defs). Overall semantic fidelity is partial_or_weakened_formalization, though most of the macro scope gap is candidly disclosed: the abstract, §4.4, §7.3, Appendix A.2, and REPRODUCIBILITY.md explicitly restrict the Lean claim to supporting algebraic identities and leave MeasureTheory expectations, moment existence, L_2(P) positive definiteness (Theorem 3.3), and asymptotic convergence outside Lean. Within the formalized algebraic slice, PATP.Param, PATP.Basis, and PATP.Degeneracy faithfully verify the quadratic exponent boundary values (0, 1/2, 1), signed-parity oddness, and pointwise basis collapse at alpha = 1/2. However, key weaknesses remain even at the algebraic level: (1) PATP.G2Algebra.g2Formula_eq_from_bFb does not formalize the 2x2 matrix F_2, its inverse, vector b, or eq. (50), nor link p to piAlpha 2 alpha—it assumes eq. (50) as the scalar quotient bFbNumerator / delta2 and proves only 1 / (c * (A / Δ)) = Δ / (c * A), slightly overstated by §4.4's 'from b^T F_2^{-1} b' wording; (2) PATP.ExponentSeparation proves the difference factorization and two root substitutions but omits the root-uniqueness conclusion of Theorem 3.2 on [0,1]; (3) PATP.Entropy (the largest Lean file, omitted from Appendix A.2 Table 2 and §7.3's 'five small modules') hardcodes tabular entropy formulas rather than integrals and proves k_Lap = e/sqrt(2) ≈ 1.9221 (docstring 1.9230), contradicting the paper's §2.5 table value 1.9300; and (4) minor junk-value/domain slips occur where piAlpha_at_zero and patpBasis_at_zero omit i ≠ 0 (relying on 1/0 = 0 at i = 0) and the paper's §3.1 claim p_i : [0,1] -> R_+ fails for i >= 6 (unverified in Lean).
- **Good Practices:** Prominent, honest scope disclosure across Abstract (line 136), §4.4 (lines 1678-1692), §7.3 (lines 2618-2625), Appendix A.2 Table 2, README.md, and REPRODUCIBILITY.md framing Lean strictly as an algebraic supplement rather than full statistical verification.; Zero `sorry`, zero `admit`, zero custom `axiom` declarations, and zero `native_decide` across all 8 Lean files.; Candid internal docstrings explicitly documenting shortcuts inside the Lean files (e.g., G2Algebra.lean:34-36 noting `inv_c_mul_div_eq` is generic; Entropy.lean:30-34 noting tabular entropy formulas are postulated; Degeneracy.lean:9-10 leaving rank and positive definiteness to manuscript assumptions).; Inclusion of 9 concrete `example` instantiations verifying hypothesis satisfiability across `Param.lean`, `Basis.lean`, and `Entropy.lean`, plus explicit domain guards on `piAlpha_at_half/one`, `patpBasis_at_zero_input`, `exponentCollisionFactor_at_second_root`, and `g2Formula_eq_from_bFb`.

- **Mode #1 — Statement & Hypothesis Drift (`minor_undisclosed`):** Moderate-to-minor domain and statement drifts exist between prose and Lean: boundary theorems omit `i ≠ 0` at `α = 0` contrary to their own docstrings; `piAlpha` is unconstrained in `α : ℝ` (avoiding the false `p_i(α) > 0` claim in §3.1 for `i ≥ 6`); `Entropy.lean` weakens scale-shift invariance (`c ≠ 0, b ∈ ℝ`) to `c > 0` without shift and proves `e/√2 ≈ 1.9221` instead of the paper table's `1.9300`; and `G2Algebra.lean` replaces positivity and distributional assumptions with `≠ 0` on abstract real scalars.
  - PATP.piAlpha_at_zero (Lean/PATP/Param.lean:32-34) and PATP.patpBasis_at_zero (Lean/PATP/Basis.lean:49-52) omit `(hi : i ≠ 0)`, even though both module/theorem docstrings (Param.lean:15, Basis.lean:43) explicitly state `i ≠ 0` is required and the paper restricts indices to i >= 1 (or i >= 2).
  - Paper §3.1 (line 958) defines `p_i(α) : [0,1] → ℝ_+` (positive codomain), which is mathematically false for i >= 6 (e.g., p_6(0.15) = 1/6 - 2.5*0.15 + (25/3)*0.15^2 = -0.02083 < 0); Lean defines `piAlpha` over unconstrained `α : ℝ` without asserting positivity and adds `(hp : 0 < piAlpha i α)` in PATP.patpBasis_at_zero_input (Basis.lean:98).
  - PATP.entropyCoefficient_scale_invariant (Lean/PATP/Entropy.lean:76-81) proves scale invariance only for `c > 0` (`hc : 0 < c`) with pre-substituted `H + Real.log c` and `c * σ`, whereas paper §2.5 (line 824) states `k(cξ + b) = k(ξ)` for all `c ≠ 0, b ∈ ℝ`.
  - Paper §2.5 table (line 863) and line 922 report Laplace entropy coefficient `k = 1.9300` (`k ≈ 1.93`), whereas PATP.entropyCoefficient_laplace (Lean/PATP/Entropy.lean:166-174) proves `Real.exp 1 / Real.sqrt 2` (≈ 1.9221; Lean docstring lines 28 and 164 write `≈ 1.9230`).
  - PATP.g2Formula_eq_from_bFb (Lean/PATP/G2Algebra.lean:54-68) weakens Theorem 4.1's positivity conditions (`c_2 > 0`, `Δ_2 > 0`) to `≠ 0` and takes an unconstrained `p : ℝ` unrelated to `piAlpha 2 α`.

  - Even within the disclosed algebraic scope, §4.4 (lines 1687-1689) says PATP.G2Algebra covers "the symbolic algebra from b^T F_2^{-1} b to the displayed rational expression", whereas Lean/PATP/G2Algebra.lean:54-68 skips eq.   - Lean/PATP/Entropy.lean (192 lines, ~40% of Lean LOC) is omitted from Appendix A.2 Table 2 and §7.3 ("five small modules", whereas Lean/PATP.lean imports 6 submodules), and omits the paper's Gaussian upper bound `k ≤ sqrt(2πe)/2` (§2.5 lines 835-839).
- **Mode #3 — Definition Smuggling (`moderate`):** Core basis definitions (`piAlpha`, `patpBasis`) faithfully use Mathlib `ℝ`, `Real.sign`, and `Real.rpow`. However, `G2Algebra` and `Entropy` avoid Mathlib's `Matrix` linear algebra and `MeasureTheory` integration by introducing standalone scalar stand-ins (`delta2`, `bFbNumerator`, hardcoded `gaussianEntropy`/`uniformEntropy`/`laplaceEntropy`), rendering `g2Formula_eq_from_bFb` a one-line fraction inversion and the canonical `k` theorems algebraic tautologies.
  - Lean/PATP/G2Algebra.lean:15-68 defines `delta2`, `bFbNumerator`, and `g2Formula` as decoupled polynomials over arbitrary scalar variables `(p c2 nupm1 nupp1 nu2p sigp : ℝ)` rather than using Mathlib `Matrix (Fin 2) (Fin 2) ℝ`, `Matrix.det`, `F_2⁻¹`, or `piAlpha 2 α`; `g2Formula_eq_from_bFb` pre-loads `bFbNumerator / delta2` into the LHS and collapses to the generic scalar identity `inv_c_mul_div_eq` (`1 / (c * (A / Δ)) = Δ / (c * A)`).
  - Lean/PATP/Entropy.lean:45-174 defines `gaussianEntropy` (lines 94-95), `uniformEntropy` (lines 101-102), and `laplaceEntropy` (lines 108-109) as hardcoded textbook log formulas ("postulated as axiomatic `def` definitions" per lines 30-34) instead of differential entropy `-∫ f ln f` via Mathlib `MeasureTheory`, and manually passes `σ = a / √12` and `σ = b √2` into `entropyCoefficient_uniform`/`entropyCoefficient_laplace`, reducing them to `exp`/`log`/`sqrt` simplifications.
