- **Mode #1 — Statement & Hypothesis Drift (`minor_disclosed`):** The headline publication statements in PaperTheorems.lean and PaperAudit.lean match the paper's Eqs. (4), (9), (11), (21), (27), (29), and (30) across all u : ℝ with exact dyadic rational constants and explicit sign bridging. Minor gaps include checking only the 0-th coordinate of the half-DC conversion, leaving deriv (y a) = yu a unstated as a theorem, and an extra a priori positivity hypothesis in the uninstantiated generic lemma DminBoundCorrectness.dmin_bound_correct.
  - PaperTheorems.lean:44-47 (phase4_n150_dc_coefficient_is_source_half) only verifies that index 0 of a_cert_ℚ equals half of the raw Float64 DC coefficient; PaperCertificate.lean:38-190 pre-halves index 0 directly rather than stating PaperTheorems via HalfDCConvention.RHalfDC on the unhalved 151-element source vector (disclosed in docs/source_notes/lean_certificate_audit_20260831.md:70-89 and Supplement §B.2).
  - Definitions.lean:237,255 and PaperAudit.lean:17 define yu a directly as the finite sine sum (-∑ k, k * a k * sin(k u)) rather than deriv (y a). While periodicHilbert (yu a) = Hyu a is formally proved (HilbertOnTrigPoly.lean:110-143), there is no explicit Lean lemma proving deriv (y a) = yu a.
  - DminBoundCorrectness.lean:87-91,170-181,335-347 (D_lipschitz_bound, d_lower_init_correct, dmin_bound_correct) strengthen hypotheses by requiring an a priori strictly positive lower bound witness (hDLower_pos : 0 < DLower) and (hcert : ∀ u, DLower ≤ D a u) in order to differentiate Real.sqrt, whereas Supplement Proposition 5.1 (docs/supplementary_computer_assisted_accuracy_proof.tex:520-543) derives the Lipschitz bound via the reverse triangle inequality in ℝ² without assuming D > 0 beforehand (and in fact uses d_lower_init to establish positivity of D). This affects only the uninstantiated generic library, not PaperTheorems.lean.
- **Mode #2 — Scope Downgrade (`moderate`):** While the paper prominently discloses that it certifies a residual bound rather than existence of an exact nearby solution and discloses in §4.3.2/Supplement/README that Lean relies on two Arb axioms, there is a moderate scope mismatch between the Abstract/Intro/§2.3.2 framing (claiming Lean verifies the analytic deductions for the candidate and substantially reduces manual auditing of the computation) and what Lean checks for the N=150 certificate: the entire ~12k-LOC analytic library sits disconnected from PaperTheorems.lean, which only executes periodic extension, sign reversal, and rational comparison on two monolithic axioms. Furthermore, default `lake build` still targets the older N=110 Main.lean rather than PaperTheorems.lean.
  - PaperCertificateAxioms.lean:16-45 and PaperTheorems.lean:17-40: For the paper's N=150 candidate, Lean does NOT instantiate AliasingErrorBound, FourierTailHilbert, TaylorRemainder, GridToSupNormTaylor, TaylorSeriesCorrectness, SeriesCorrectness, DminBoundCorrectness, or ResidualAssemblyCorrectness with intermediate Arb data.   - Abstract (2611.07395.md:79), Introduction (2611.07395.md:108), and §2.3.2 (2611.07395.md:315-317) state that Lean 'machine-checks the deductions connecting the certified numerical bounds to the final residual theorem', 'substantially reducing the amount of computational reasoning that must be manually audited', and 'verifies the analytic deductions and periodic extension' for the reported candidate—whereas only §4.3.2 (2611.07395.md:684), Supplement §B.4 (lines 1026-1055), and Lean/README.md (lines 25-30, 50-83) clarify that the generic analytic library is not instantiated on the N=150 certificate.
